We're building tools that make complex things simple. Not by hiding the complexity, but by designing around it — so the right people can move faster without losing clarity. Most software asks you to adapt to it. We think it should be the other way around. The details matter: the moment something clicks, the friction you never notice until it's gone, the interface that gets out of the way. That's what we're working toward. Slowly, deliberately, and with a lot of iteration along the way.
